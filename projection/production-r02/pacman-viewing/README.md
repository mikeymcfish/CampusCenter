- [Compact viewing, route/barrier proof and reviewed frames](https://github.com/mikeymcfish/CampusCenter/releases/download/campuscenter-pacman-r02-compact-viewing-20261006/CampusCenter_UV02_PacMan_R02_Compact_Viewing_And_Barrier_Proof.zip) (1,964,819 bytes).


The 96-second H.264 MP4 is a **camera viewing preview**, 1024x846 including banner, 20fps/1920 frames. Open in a video player; do not use these pixels as a projection atlas. One actor follows one continuous closed route, with no portals/teleports or geometry edits, through 24/24 accessible reference selections and 11/13 H02 stops. Locker121 is entrance-only. Visits do not imply full floor coverage; reference labels are frozen coarse masks, not a fresh semantic-boundary audit.

ASPIRE and Collaboration are omitted because the printed receiver contains door, wall and glazing barriers, leaving seams too narrow for the 2.6mm body. Continuous polygon checks find access for a 0.25mm diameter disk but not a 0.5mm diameter disk or the character, even without clearance. The full route reserves a 1.85mm-radius visual footprint plus 0.3mm clearance. This is a **printed-model geometry/character-size result**, not a claim that real architectural rooms are sealed or inaccessible to people.

Route segments were rechecked with the restored fireplace. The matched UV02 assembly is five original P02 1:100 tiles plus P03 B3 only. At this compact full-model view the body spans about3.67 pixels; physical readability and actual MadMapper playback are untested. Producer reviewed four decoded camera/native frames; publisher verified exact video hash/size, metadata and full decoding. Public kit sanitizes private provenance without changing video/image bytes.

Raw atlas test files are separate; this small release contains neither the 8192-square lossless atlas movie nor complete atlas sequence. P01 multi-actor prototype/history remains preserved. Whole-building Ripple and remaining caption pagination remain pending. No Library retry or rebuild occurred.
