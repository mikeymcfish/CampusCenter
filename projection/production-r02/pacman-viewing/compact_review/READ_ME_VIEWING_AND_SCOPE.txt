PAC-MAN R02 COMPACT VIEWING CHECKPOINT

PacMan_R02_SingleActor_Mapped_1024_Viewing_Preview.mp4: small standard H.264 camera-view preview; 1024 x 846 including banner, 96 seconds, 20 fps.
Review this video as a view of the assembled model. Its pixels are not the raw UV atlas used as projection media.

The separate 8192 x 8192 lossless PNG MOV and PNG sequence are the raw atlas deliverables. They are not included in this small supplemental package.

ASPIRE and Collaboration are omitted because the unchanged printed receiver contains door, wall and glazing barriers around those floor selections. The remaining geometric seams are too narrow for the 2.6 mm diameter character body. A continuous polygon check finds access for a 0.25 mm diameter disk, but no access for a 0.5 mm diameter disk or the actual character, even without clearance. The full route also reserves a larger 1.85 mm radius visual footprint plus 0.3 mm clearance. This is a model geometry and character-size result; it does not claim the real architectural rooms are sealed or inaccessible to people. The selection names still come from frozen coarse H02 reference masks.

One actor follows one continuous closed route through 24 accessible reference selections and 11 of 13 H02 stops. Locker 121 is visited at its entrance only. Visits do not mean complete floor coverage.
Every saved continuous route segment has been checked against the actual unchanged receiver, including the restored fireplace. No portals, teleports, or geometry edits were used.
At this compact full-model resolution the body spans about 3.67 pixels. Real projector readability and MadMapper playback have not been tested.
No Library retry, GitHub push, installation, print job, or MadMapper project/cue edit was performed.
