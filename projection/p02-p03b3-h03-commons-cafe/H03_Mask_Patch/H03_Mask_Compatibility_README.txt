H03 is an atlas-mask revision for the unchanged UV02 1:100 hybrid receiver. Use complete H03 atlases directly, or multiply your original receiver colour by the chosen alpha mask and composite the existing neutral UV02 fireplace patch on its separate new-chart mask. Do not apply a camera viewing MP4 as a projection atlas. Original H02/P01/R02 files remain unchanged. See World_Clipped_Mask_Manifest.json and H03_Mask_Patch_Reference.json for exact receiver hashes, scopes, exclusions and AA method. Physical projection is untested.
