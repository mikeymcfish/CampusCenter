CampusCenter - Tour static test images - Production P01

This folder matches the included Model/CampusCenter_P02_Assembly_1_100_UV01.obj, physical 1:100.
OBJ numeric coordinates are millimetres, right-handed Z up. The OBJ is an
exact byte copy of the frozen P02 UV01 source. No vertices, faces, corner
UVs, world origin or physical scale changed. The copied MTL points all
three named regions to the Student Commons atlas as a convenient default;
the source MTL is unchanged. Independent material slots are not required.

Load and test
1. Use the included exact OBJ for the MadMapper 3D surface, retaining its
   original UV coordinates and established physical registration.
2. Add one *_Atlas_8192.png to the Media Bin and assign it as the texture
   for the complete model. Use the full image, without crop, stretch or
   UV adjustment. All model regions share this one atlas; glass and loops
   have black atlas regions. Leave additional shading disabled.
3. Test Student Commons, Innovation Lab, ASPIRE and Cafe by switching the
   atlas media. Use the labelled *_Mapped_Preview.png and the actual native
   *_Native_Emission_Preview.png to check placement and text orientation.
   Those preview files and *_Gym_Caption_Reference.png are NOT atlases.
4. Keep nearest/point sampling when available. The atlas is 8192 square;
   import it at native resolution. Do not add a second light/shadow pass.
5. Calibrate the 3D surface to the actual model and projector. This delivery
   assumes a single projector approximately normal to the floor plane from
   +Z. It does not claim physical projection or hidden-wall illumination.

The four named rooms use their approved H02 masks. Titles and body copy
are typed verbatim from the authoritative copy, entirely within the gym
floor with a physical inset. There are no room numbers or diagnostic labels
in presentation textures. The title/body are upright toward world +Y.
Furniture tops and sides use contrasting colours, one fixed light direction,
and geometry-derived contact shading. All other rooms remain black.

This is specifically the 100 scale. A different physical scale requires
its own UV01 OBJ and atlas package; the 1:250 sample is a print adaptation
with a distinct atlas, not a uniformly shrunken assembly.

QA: fresh native Blender OBJ import rechecked exact mesh coordinates and
UVs, reapplied every atlas to all parts with one shared Emission shader,
and rendered without lights. Independent world-to-original-UV raster previews
also verify the caption inset and placement. Original source/H01/H02 hashes
are retained in the handoff. Source copying and MTL presentation binding
do not modify the original model files.

No MadMapper project, cue programming, software installation, print job,
