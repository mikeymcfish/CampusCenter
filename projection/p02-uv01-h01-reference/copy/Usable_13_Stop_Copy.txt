King School Campus Center
CRI and Campus Center Space Descriptions for Virtual Reality Walkthrough

Entry + Reception
The Entry sets the tone for the Campus Center as a welcoming gateway into a space built for learning and connection. It is the first point of entry for students, families, and visitors to King’s Campus Center and everything it offers.

The Reception Area offers a warm and welcoming first point of connection for everyone entering the Campus Center.

The Cafe
The Cafe is designed as a natural gathering place where students, faculty, and visitors can connect and recharge. More than a place to grab something to eat, it supports the relationships and informal conversations that strengthen King’s collaborative and caring community.

The Student Commons
The Student Commons is the vibrant heart of the Campus Center, giving students a comfortable place to gather, study, relax, and connect around its welcoming fireplace. The space reflects King’s belief that a strong sense of belonging supports both personal growth and meaningful learning.

Admissions Office
The Admissions Office is a place where prospective Upper School students and families begin to imagine themselves as part of the King community. Through personal conversations and authentic connections, prospective students and families can discover a school that values curiosity, individuality, belonging, and the confidence to pursue a purposeful future.

CRI Gallery
The CRI Gallery makes learning visible by showcasing student research, creative work, discoveries, and ideas from across disciplines. It celebrates the process of inquiry as much as the final product, inviting the community to ask questions, make connections, and be inspired by what King students are exploring.

Corridor / Display Hall
The Display Hall transforms a passageway into an evolving story of learning at King. Student work, research, accomplishments, and creative thinking turns each walk through the space into a chance to discover new ideas and celebrate the community's diverse talents.

Innovation Lab
The Innovation Lab is a flexible learning environment where ideas can move from imagination to prototype through designing, building, testing, and rethinking. In this space, Students collaborate across disciplines, use emerging tools and technologies, and develop the creative confidence and perseverance needed to solve real-world problems.

ASPIRE Seminar Room
The Advanced Science Program for Independent Research and Engineering (ASPIRE) Seminar Room supports King’s advanced student researchers as they investigate complex questions, analyze evidence, and present original scientific work. It is a space for deep inquiry, mentorship, collaboration, and the kind of high-level research experience that prepares students to contribute confidently beyond King.

Collaboration Room
The Collaboration Room gives humanities students and educators a focused space to think together and exchange perspectives. Whether used for project teams, mentoring, research discussions, or interdisciplinary planning, it supports the dialogue and collaborative problem-solving at the center of humanities-based research.

Trophy Hall / Corridor
The Trophy Hall celebrates the dedication, teamwork, and school spirit that generations of Vikings have brought to King athletics. As students pass through, it connects past accomplishments with the perseverance, leadership, and commitment that continue to shape today’s teams.

Athletics Office
The Athletics Office is a hub for the leadership, coordination, and relationships that support King’s student-athletes and teams. It reflects an approach to athletics that develops confidence, teamwork, responsibility, and growth as part of the whole student experience.

Athletics Trainers’ Room
The Athletic Trainers’ Room supports student-athlete health, safety, recovery, and readiness to return to the activities King’s athletes love. The space reflects King’s commitment to wellness and to caring for students as whole people while helping them pursue their goals with resilience and confidence.

Gym
The existing Gym remains a lively center for competition, teamwork, school spirit, and community traditions. From practices and games to shared celebrations, it is a place where students challenge themselves, support one another, and experience the perseverance, respect, and integrity that define the Viking community.
