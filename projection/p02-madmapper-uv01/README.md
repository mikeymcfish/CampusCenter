Source OBJ vertex/normal decimals, triangle topology/winding, coordinates, origin, millimetre units and right-handed Z-up axes are unchanged from P02. UV01 replaces old planar UVs with separate surface charts on an 8192 square atlas. Opposing wall faces are independent; default receiver masks omit glazing, loops and undersides while retaining their geometry. Build independent room/surface layers in the common input atlas; material slot names alone do not create separate controls. Old planar content needs remapping.

Producer fresh Blender imports, atlas coverage, positive unmirrored triangle areas and no positive-area overlaps passed. Diagnostic images and public QA are included. Physical projector calibration, print fit, software-specific MadMapper operation and show acceptance remain untested. This delivery contains model/UV resources, not a MadMapper project, animation or cue set.

Stairs A/B, revised room boundaries, open Commons/cafe/display-hall boundaries and shared wall-cap ownership remain ambiguous. Neutral EXTERIOR/UNDERSIDE IDs retain unassigned surfaces. Confirm or revise these masks before using them as final show controls; names do not establish ownership.
