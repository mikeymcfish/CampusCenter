## Unreal engine content

- Niagara Fluids fire template and the engine basic-shape assets depend on the installed Unreal Engine distribution and its license. They are not CC0 standalone meshes. Use Unreal migration to retain asset references; do not distribute an isolated dependency-incomplete uasset.
