# Reference and equipment sources

School architectural, iLab, landscape and survey PDFs are retained as project references. Render images are appearance references. Inclusion does not change their original ownership or licensing.

- Formlabs space-planning CAD: https://forum.formlabs.com/t/3d-models-of-formlabs-printers-space-planning-models/46063/5 . Original STEP files are retained in `equipment/source_cad`. Manufacturer download availability is not an unrestricted redistribution license.
- OLSK representative vinyl cutter: https://github.com/Open-Lab-Starter-Kit/OLSK-Vinyl-Cutter . Its CERN-OHL-W-2.0 and CC-BY-SA-4.0 texts accompany the source CAD. The school plan does not identify a cutter make.
- Kenney computer peripherals: https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit . CC0; license retained in `equipment/licenses`.
- Bambu Lab H2D: https://eu.store.bambulab.com/products/h2d ; AMS 2 Pro: https://us.store.bambulab.com/products/ams-2-pro . Nominal planning envelopes are used. Generated reconstructed surfaces are not manufacturer CAD.
- Epson F2100: https://files.support.epson.com/docid/cpd5/cpd54903.pdf . Nominal dimensions informed the model.
- WAZER: https://wazer.com/waterjets/tech-specs/ . Nominal dimensions informed the model.
- BigRep ONE: https://bigrep.com/bigrep-one/ . The specific school machine revision is unconfirmed.
- UV flatbed and spray booth: representative plan-based equipment, not confirmed school makes. Their photo references and image-generation inputs are retained.
- Simplified generated equipment: reference-informed images followed by `microsoft/TRELLIS.2`, then simplification and removal of textures. Rear H2D geometry was repaired as a plain panel. Current integrated geometry is in the Blender scene and equipment library.
- LEGO/LDraw: https://www.ldraw.org/article/218.html and https://library.ldraw.org/ . The project LDraw file references standard parts; it does not bundle the third-party part library. The Blender LEGO preview uses schematic blocks and studs. This is an unofficial building guide.
