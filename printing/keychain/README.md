# Two-color keychain

Open Campus_Center_Two_Color.3mf as a multi-part object. Assign Black_structure to black filament and White_glazing to white filament. The 3MF contains color metadata, but confirm filament assignments in your slicer.

Alternatively import BOTH STL files together as parts of ONE object; keep their shared coordinates. Do not independently arrange or drop the glazing onto the bed. White is solid infill, not transparent material or empty windows. Print the flat underside down. The integral loop and original silhouette are retained. No supports should be needed for the combined solid; the separated white file is not intended to print by itself.

Window bays and mullions are broadened for this roughly 61 mm keychain. Use a fine layer height and preview the small white features in your slicer. The solids have been checked for manifold validity, zero overlap and volume preservation; no physical print was made.
