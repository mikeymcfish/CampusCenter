Fire fills the central 80 percent of the width and lower 85 percent of height. Pure black completely featureless background to all four edges, no fireplace surround, no stone, no room, no floor, no reflections, no lettering, no smoke plume. Entire logs and all flames contained within the frame, generous black margin at top and sides for clean billboard compositing. Carefully exposed photographic detail in the flame, no clipped white blobs, no fantasy effects. This will be an emissive image plane INSIDE an existing fireplace opening, not a photo of a whole fireplace.
