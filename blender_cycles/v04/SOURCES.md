# v04 asset sources

Downloaded 2026-09-08. These plant and environment assets are CC0:

- [Potted Plant 01 - Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/potted_plant_01): 2K Blender model and dependencies; shared instances for the commons.
- [Shrub 03 - Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/shrub_03): 2K model and textures; varied groundcover in the entry containers. Illustrative species selection.
- [Grass Medium 01 - Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/grass_medium_01): 2K model and textures; terrace planting and ground blades.
- [Greenwich Park - Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/greenwich_park): 4K HDR panorama, Andreas Mischok. Illustrative background, not the school grounds.
- [Grass 001 - ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Grass001): 2K JPG PBR set; approximately 1.4 m surface scale. Color is blended at two orientations to reduce large-area repetition. Download: https://ambientcg.com/get?file=Grass001_2K-JPG.zip .

Poly Haven downloads were checked against API-provided MD5 values; SHA-256 and source URLs are recorded in `assets/DOWNLOADS.json`. The delivery manifest covers every included asset, including the additional ambientCG set.

The fire insert is an original image created by the built-in Imagegen tool. It is used directly, without pixel editing, as an emissive image plane. Full prompt: `FIRE_PROMPT.md`.

Landscape plan references remain in the main repository: `references/plans/Landscape Plans  King School Campus Center.PDF` (L-200 and L-400). The v04 paving/lawn is simplified visual context rather than a replacement for the definitive plan. Earlier King identity and inherited material ownership/provenance remain as documented in v03 and v01.
