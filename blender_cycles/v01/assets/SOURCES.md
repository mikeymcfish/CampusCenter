# Texture sources

All three ambientCG assets below are CC0. The files were obtained for the Unreal material pass and reused here. This Blender file packs the images and explicitly converts DirectX normal maps to Blender's normal-map convention.

- Wood049: https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Wood049 — 2K Color, NormalDX and Roughness; 0.8 m tile.
- Fabric030: https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Fabric030 — 1K Color, NormalDX and Roughness; tinted to the existing sage furniture palette.
- PaintedPlaster017: https://ambientcg.com/view?id=PaintedPlaster017 — 1K Color, NormalDX and Roughness; tinted warm off-white.

The original masonry and paving textures remain packed in the source scene. Their mapping and normal intensity were refined; they were not replaced with unrelated architectural finishes.
