# Campus Center Cycles Studio v05

Open `Campus_Center_Cycles_Studio.blend`. All textures are packed. This version builds on v04; the original scene and walkthrough remain intact.

- Three King banners enlarged another 25% in width and height, now 1.75 x 5.25 m.
- Eight original Imagegen designs used in 36 wall placements: club meetups, food drive signup, maker studio, robotics/repair, two charcoal portraits, watercolor landscape and gouache still life. Mixed sizes, frames and taped paper across both hallway levels, commons and iLab.
- New modern fireplace: 2.8 m charcoal mineral surround, 3.4 m limestone hearth, oak mantel, recessed metal firebox and widened photographic flame insert. The flame remains a static billboard.
- New art and fireplace are in separate V05 collections for convenient visibility control. Superseded artwork and small fire insert are retained but hidden.

The artwork is fictional student-style decoration, not actual student submissions or announcements of real events. Fireplace detailing is a visual design interpretation, not construction documentation.

`assets/` contains the eight unmodified Imagegen outputs. `PROMPTS.md` records prompts and provenance. Earlier packed textures and HDRI retain their v04 provenance in the sibling v04 package.

`stills/` contains eight 1920 x 1080 Cycles views (256 maximum adaptive samples, CUDA, denoising, inherited AgX), 16-bit RGB PNG and multilayer EXR, plus JPEG previews and `Eight_Views.jpg`. Preview renders are separate and are not final deliverables.

Validation reopens the saved file, checks source preservation, retained original mesh topology, banner size, artwork count, packed image dependencies and original walkthrough. Image validation checks final dimensions and EXR passes; views are also visually inspected.
