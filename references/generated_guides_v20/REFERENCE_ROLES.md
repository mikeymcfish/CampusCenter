## Future nine-image budget

Use up to three scene/frame anchors, two overall interior-look references, and three or four item-detail references (eight or nine total). Adjust that mix per clip. Candidate item details: Bambu Lab H2D printer, AMS 2 Pro, King banner/crest and a scene-specific technology/furniture item. No additional item images are being generated in this three-image batch.

In each video prompt, identify every reference by its actual attachment label and role. The video controls movement, viewpoint, room location and blocking. Item photos control only the named item's design, proportions, controls, branding and finish; they must not change object placement or building layout. Overall look references control color, furniture appearance, people styling and decoration. Frame references guide appearance at their timestamps and must not override video motion or the definitive floor plans.

Use detail references only when their item is visible or relevant to the clip. Do not imply a reference has been supplied until it is actually attached. Use an exact readable King banner image when lettering/crest accuracy matters.
