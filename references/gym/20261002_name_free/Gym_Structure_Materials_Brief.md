## Main structures to reproduce
- High exposed roof, not a drop ceiling. Corrugated/ribbed metal roof deck is warm off-white with fine repeating long ribs. Exact roof pitch/ridge cannot be established reliably from these camera views.
- Repeated parallel open-web steel trusses. Thin white/off-white upper and lower chords, alternating triangular diagonal webs, some vertical web elements. Broad lower chords read as paired slender rails rather than enormous solid concrete beams. Secondary perpendicular purlins support the deck. Do not replace this with a generic black warehouse skeleton.
- Main roof/perimeter members and wall-top framing are off-white. Exposed vertical steel/wall piers interrupt the upper sidewalls. There are small shadows, seams and mild grime, not heavy rust.
- Large rectangular white HVAC trunks/boxes pass between/above trusses. Short broad cylindrical necks terminate in conspicuous white circular concentric-ring diffusers, with dark annular gaps and a central disc. They are not large exposed silver spiral ducts. Smaller white cylindrical pipes also run around the perimeter.
- Rectangular skylights in repeated roof bays. They show pale blue-white daylight, slim frames and occasional cross-bracing through the view. Bright electric lights are compact suspended rectangular fixtures, usually two luminous parallel strips; thin chains/wires suspend them beneath the deck/trusses.
- Black pipework forms a visible overhead network with elbows, tees and drops. Keep its visual distinction from white HVAC and structural steel. It resembles sprinkler/service piping, but that function is inferred rather than proven.
- A long navy rolled divider curtain traverses the middle overhead. It has repeated dark clamp/bracket supports, a cylindrical roll profile, an end motor/gear assembly and hanging cables. Do not interpret it as a huge primary navy beam.
- Multiple retractable basketball systems hang high/folded in the supplied views: transparent glass backboards, dark navy padded rims of boards, black tubular articulated triangular support arms, silver/gray braces, red-orange hoops and white nets. Some brackets fix to the wall on wood mounting plates. Their folded positions matter in ceiling comparisons.

## Wall construction and furnishing
- Upper walls are painted off-white concrete masonry units. Horizontal mortar/block joints remain visible with mild uneven paint and wear. They are not a smooth featureless plaster plane.
- Exposed lower blockwork is deep navy/near-black. End walls have separate tall navy vinyl-padded panels, each with clear vertical seams and slight soft wrinkles/scuffs. The large central KING lettering is warm muted gold and spans multiple pads.
- Two end walls both carry KING padding. Distinguish them by the banner groups: the recent 2024–26 end has 1865 in the navy floor baseline; the opposite scoreboard end has the 2008–14 group and scoreboard.
- The team-bench sideline has folded navy chairs, a gray double egress door, scorer's table with KING branding and a navy/gold Vikings shield on the wall. Its long championship strip runs from 2018 toward 2024, with a deliberate shield-sized gap.
- Opposite bench wall: varnished honey-colored retractable wood bleachers, dark supports, black tubular handrails and dark stair/step faces. Some bleachers are folded flat against the wall; others extend in the DNG preview. Keep wood slats/rows and black rails distinct.
- A tall projecting white wall bay interrupts the bleacher wall. Two beige double doors with metal kickplates sit below a small elevated white-railed platform. The platform carries a navy KING cloth banner; beneath it are off-white enclosed soffit brackets, conduit and a red AED cabinet on dark blockwork. Small protective wire cages cover wall fixtures by the doors.
- Volleyball net is installed across the court: white top/bottom tape, dark fine square netting, repeated KING VIKINGS top-tape lettering, navy padded posts and a padded referee stand/ladder. This is movable sports equipment, not part of the permanent wall geometry.
- U.S. flag hangs at the corner between bench sideline and scoreboard end. The physical scoreboard is navy/blue with white static labels; its LED digits are dark in the photos. Retain any separately authorized scoreboard display styling rather than treating the unlit reference as a requested score change.

## Floor materials and markings
- The main finish is clear-coated narrow-strip maple: pale honey/golden tan, considerable light/dark board variation, fine lengthwise grain, staggered short end joints. It is not orange parquet, broad rustic planks, or a flat yellow texture.
- Boards read lengthwise along the long gym/court direction. At close range use real narrow-strip frequency; avoid giant grain scaled across the room. Grain and seams should remain visible beneath painted/stained graphics.
- Gloss is strong enough to show crisp rectangular skylight/light reflections and softened KING reflections, but not a mirror-perfect uniformly wet floor. Preserve fine scuffs, subdued scratches and variable highlights. Recommended initial renderer roughness approximately 0.18–0.30, to be tuned under matched lighting; this is a modelling starting range, not a measurement.
- Primary basketball lines and court-edge strips are deep navy: boundary, centerline/circle, lane outlines and broad arcs. Volleyball markings are thin off-white/very pale cool white rectangles. Additional auxiliary arcs/lines are light gray. Do not recolor every sports line to the same white.
- Wide navy sideline strip contains large gold/wood-tone HOME OF THE VIKINGS lettering. Opposite end baseline includes 1865. Near the bleacher doors is a separate navy rectangle with a large gold K.
- Large center-court Viking-ship crest: navy varsity-like K centered in a navy circle, warm translucent brown/gold wooden ship/scroll silhouette, three burgundy-red sail shapes with gold stars, and paired navy wave motifs outside. The crest keeps visible wood grain; it is not a solid yellow modern Viking head or a plain K-only center court. Perspective means use the source for shape, not a guessed texture projection.

## Championship banner assets
There are 66 unique team championship banners in the seven views. The structured companion manifest has exact observed line breaks, wall/row/column positions and source filenames. Sixty-two are fully verified for generation; four class letters on the oblique bleacher wall remain held. Preserve genuine acronym differences: WNEPSFHA field hockey, WNEPSGSBA 2023 softball and WNEPSAA 2008/2010 softball.

Most banners: navy rectangular cloth, narrow off-white/cream binding, slightly irregular hem, corner mounting points, restrained sag and folds. Type is centered, bold cream-gold outlined collegiate block capitals, crowded but readable. Three older banners at the recent end use burgundy/red letters with pale white outlines. Avoid adding shields, school logos, ribbons, decorative emblems or unobserved years to the plain championship cloths.

Names, individual-award displays and the red handwritten athlete rosters are intentionally excluded. Do not invent names or imitate roster text with generated gibberish. Use plain off-white wall areas there for this scoped reconstruction.
